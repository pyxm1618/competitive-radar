# Xiaoshuyin context (for the Intelligence Agent)

Keep this short on purpose. Do not copy the Xiaoshuyin product into this repository.

## What Xiaoshuyin is

An internal product effort focused on family problem-solving support: helping a
household discover a problem, understand it, act on it together, and keep
following up over time. The Competitive Radar exists to support **product
design decisions**, not to write competitor profiles.

## The one thing to remember

> The goal is not to describe what a competitor did.
> The goal is to judge whether a competitor change has meaning for Xiaoshuyin's
> product design.

Every intelligence record must answer, explicitly:

- What changed (only what the evidence supports)?
- What underlying product mechanism does it exercise?
- Does that mechanism matter to Xiaoshuyin, and why?
- What should the product owner do now (act / investigate / watch / ignore)?

## Capability areas Xiaoshuyin cares about

Family problem discovery · assessment / formulation · AI support · action
planning · parent-child collaboration · execution of actions · long-term
tracking · follow-up · reflection · reporting · professional / expert support ·
commercialization.

These map 1:1 to `config/topics.yaml`. Classify against that taxonomy only.

## What this repository must NOT become

- A Xiaoshuyin product codebase.
- A place to reconstruct the full Xiaoshuyin spec.
- A general competitor directory or a BI dashboard.

## Guardrails

- Never invent a Xiaoshuyin feature, roadmap item, or product decision.
- Low evidence means low confidence, even when the topic feels important.
- `impact` (how much it would matter if true) and `confidence` (how well the
  evidence supports it) are independent. `high / low` is a valid combination.
